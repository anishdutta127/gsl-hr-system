## Vision

### 10x Check (surfaced for decision, not forced)

The brief lands in a pipeline-Kanban-plus-forms tool, which is table stakes. The 10x version is the **candidate-facing story** — a hiring system that a 22-year-old candidate recommends to their friend because the experience was warm, clear, and mobile. That recommendation surface is the Mafatlal differentiator. Internal ops Kanban becomes hygiene; candidate portal + prompt library + leadership dashboard become the platform story.

### Platonic Ideal

A candidate finds a GSL role via Google, applies at `/careers` from their phone during lunch, takes the assessment on the same phone before the bus home, records a 60-second video from their sofa, gets a magic link the next day with their HOD interview slot, signs the offer digitally, starts Day 1 with an onboarding checklist that's already ticked off by the first cup of chai. HR sits in a Kanban that feels like Linear, not like Excel. HOD drags candidates through their role-configured rubric. Leadership opens a dashboard and sees time-in-stage p95 per role, conversion per source, withdrawal reasons — the actual signals that tell them whether hiring is healthy. Every prompt in the AI library is a tool HR reaches for reflexively, not documentation they forgot exists.

## Accepted Scope (added to Phase 1 plan)

- Full candidate portal experience: progress visualisation with ETA per stage, welcome + recruiter-contact card, save-progress between sessions, post-offer celebration moment.
- Mobile-first design sequence for the candidate portal (375px primary, desktop adapts up).
- In-app prompt drawer: sidebar "Prompts" section with title + use-case + copy button + schema preview + example output + favourite.
- JSON output contract per prompt: schema declared, validator enforced on paste-back, loud error on malformed output.
- Quality floor discipline: launch with only the prompts that are HR-validated; skeletons don't ship.
- HOD rubric: `role.rubric = [{criterion, weight, scale}]`, per-role configurable. HOD scores each criterion plus freeform notes. Feeds into Interview.scores.
- Candidate self-withdraw button in portal with optional reason, audit entry, HR notification.
